CODELAUNCH TECHNOLOGIES
PROJECT PLANNING CHECKLIST

Use this checklist to prepare for a software project discovery conversation.
You do not need to have every answer before getting started.

1. THE PROBLEM
[ ] What user or business problem are we solving?
[ ] Who experiences the problem, and how do they handle it today?
[ ] What outcome would make the project successful?
[ ] Are there deadlines, launch dates, or regulatory constraints?

2. USERS AND SCOPE
[ ] Who will use the product? What roles and permissions are needed?
[ ] What are the most important user journeys for the first release?
[ ] What is explicitly out of scope for the first release?
[ ] What accessibility, language, or device requirements apply?

3. SYSTEMS AND DATA
[ ] Which existing applications, APIs, or databases must connect?
[ ] Are API specifications, test environments, or technical contacts available?
[ ] What data is sensitive, regulated, or subject to retention rules?
[ ] Who owns the data, and where may it be stored or processed?

4. SECURITY AND RELIABILITY
[ ] How should users and services authenticate?
[ ] What authorization roles, audit trails, or approval steps are required?
[ ] What availability, response-time, backup, and recovery targets matter?
[ ] How will secrets be stored, rotated, and kept out of source control?

5. DELIVERY AND OPERATIONS
[ ] Which cloud, hosting, and technology constraints already exist?
[ ] What environments are needed (development, test, staging, production)?
[ ] Who reviews releases, and what are the deployment and rollback steps?
[ ] Who will monitor, support, and maintain the solution after launch?

6. NEXT STEPS
[ ] Identify a business decision maker and technical point of contact.
[ ] Gather relevant diagrams, contracts, sample data, and existing documentation.
[ ] Agree on the first measurable milestone and how it will be accepted.
[ ] List open questions, assumptions, risks, and dependencies.
